Privacy Policy for HighlightAI

Last updated: October 11, 2026

HighlightAI - Powered by Gemini ("the Extension") is designed with your privacy as a top priority.

1. Information Collection and Storage

The Extension does not collect, store, or share any personal user data on external servers.

  • API Keys: Your Gemini API Key is stored exclusively on your local device using Chrome's built-in secure storage (chrome.storage.local). It is never transmitted to any third-party servers.
  • Highlighted Text: When you use the extension, the text you select is sent directly from your browser to Google's official Gemini API endpoints for processing. We do not store, log, or read your text or the AI responses.

2. Third-Party Services

The Extension communicates directly with the official Google Gemini API to generate text summaries and breakdowns. Your usage is governed by Google's terms and privacy practices.

3. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
